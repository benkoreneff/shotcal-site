Carpe

Carpe is a personal macOS menu bar app that turns a screenshot into a calendar event. It works with Apple Calendar or Google Calendar. You press a keyboard shortcut and drag over an event on your screen, such as an invitation in an email or a date on a web page. Carpe reads the title, date, time and place from the image and adds the event to the calendar you chose.

Privacy Policy

Carpe is a personal project and is not affiliated with Apple, Google or Anthropic.
